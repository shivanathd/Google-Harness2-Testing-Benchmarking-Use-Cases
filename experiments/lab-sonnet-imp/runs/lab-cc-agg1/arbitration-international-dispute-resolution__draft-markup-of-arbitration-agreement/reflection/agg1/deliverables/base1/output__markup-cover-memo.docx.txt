## 3. Points Where the Playbook Needs Clarification

1.  **Expedited sub-deadlines conflict (§17).** A 10-business-day appointment followed by a hearing up to 30 days after appointment can place the hearing about day 44, leaving about one day before the 45-day award. The draft keeps the playbook terms and brackets an outside hearing date in §5.5(b). Recommend setting that date (for example, before day 30 after filing) so the 45-day award is achievable. The 60-day fallback would also fix the timing but is a concession. Please choose.
2.  **Confidentiality exceptions vs. LP reporting (§9 vs. §20).** Section 9 allows only three exceptions and omits LP reporting. Section 20 cites LP reporting as a reason to keep arbitration records. Cascadian may ask for an LP-reporting exception, so please decide whether Whitmore wants to request one.
3.  **Class waiver vs. consolidation (§16 vs. §11).** The playbook's required waiver language bars "consolidated" proceedings, which conflicts with consensual consolidation. The draft adds a last sentence in §7.3 preserving consented consolidation. Please confirm.
4.  **Qualification tier (§5 vs. §22).** Section 5 says "Required," but §22 lists qualifications under "Recommended." We treated them as Required.
