## 5. Open Issues to Resolve Before Sending

1.  **Expedited timeline does not fit internally.** Section 5.4 requires appointment within 10 business days (about 14 calendar days), a hearing within 30 days of appointment (day 44), and a final award within 45 days (day 45). That leaves about one day for a reasoned award with findings of fact and conclusions of law, which is not realistic. Options: (a) keep the 45-day award and shorten the hearing window to about 14 days after appointment (hearing around day 28, leaving about two weeks for post-hearing submissions and the award); or (b) take the 60-day fallback, which keeps the 30-day hearing window and leaves about 16 days for the award. We recommend option (a) if Cascadian's counsel will accept it, since it keeps the client's 45-day priority intact.
2.  **Class waiver vs. consolidation (§2.3 vs. §7.1).** The playbook's required language says no party may bring or participate in a "consolidated" proceeding, which would bar even consented consolidation under §7.1. The markup uses the required language as drafted and flags the tension. Decide whether to keep the bar on class-type consolidation only.
3.  **Discovery classification.** Playbook §8 says Required; §22 says Recommended. We have treated it as Required. Please confirm with GC.
4.  **Co-Investment Agreement not in the data room.** Playbook §6 requires the CIA to be Delaware-governed too. The CIA's notice provision (§11.4, cross-referenced in §12.1) and its arbitration provisions (referenced in §12.6, which says the Exhibit controls over conflicting CIA terms) have not been reviewed. Please provide the CIA before the redline goes out.
5.  **Internal notes must be removed before circulation.** The bracketed notes cite the playbook, fallback positions, and GC-only instructions. They must be stripped before the redline goes to Pinnacle Winterhaven or Cascadian. We have not prepared a clean external version; we can do so on request.
6.  **Timing.** The first D. Ostrander email set the draft for Jan. 11; later emails set Jan. 12. Final to Pinnacle Winterhaven is Jan. 13 under both. Please confirm the timeline.
